The Hi-Lo Card Counting System Explained

The Hi-Lo system is the most widely tested and accessible card counting method in blackjack. It operates as a level-one balanced count, meaning that across a full 52-card deck, the sum of all assigned values equals exactly zero.

Card Values Tag / Count Value Impact on Remaining Shoe
2, 3, 4, 5, 6 +1 Low cards favor the dealer; their removal tilts advantage toward the player.
7, 8, 9 0 Neutral cards with minimal effect on overall odds.
10, J, Q, K, Ace -1 High cards favor the player (3:2 blackjacks and double downs); their removal reduces player edge.

Running Count vs. True Count

Keeping the running count is step one: as each card is dealt face up, add or subtract its assigned tag value. However, in games using multiple decks, the running count alone does not reflect your true mathematical advantage. You must calculate the True Count:

True Count = Running Count ÷ Estimated Decks Remaining

For instance, if your running count is +6 and there are 2 decks left in the shoe, your True Count is +3. A True Count of +2 or higher typically signals the moment to increase bet sizing, as the shoe has a rich concentration of 10s and Aces.

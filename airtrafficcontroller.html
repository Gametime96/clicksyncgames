Surviving the Air Traffic Control Radar

Step into the shoes of a tower dispatcher in our Air Traffic Control simulator. Your objective is simple but highly stressful: maintain safe separation between inbound and outbound aircraft and guide them to their designated runways without a collision.

Managing a radar screen requires constant situational awareness. You must adjust the speed and heading of multiple commercial jets simultaneously. Prioritize aircraft with low fuel, maintain visual spacing, and keep a cool head as the airspace becomes increasingly crowded. Good luck, Controller!

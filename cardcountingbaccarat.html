RULES

How to Play:

Closest hand to 9 points wins.

Player and Banker win bets pay 1:1.

Tie pays 8:1; Player/Banker push.

Aces=1; 2–9=Face; 10/J/Q/K=0.

Third cards are auto-dealt rules.

HOW TO COUNT IN BACCARAT

Track cards revealed face-up in the shoe using the following values:

Card Denomination Count Value Impact
Ace, 2, 3 +1 Favors Player as low cards deplete
5, 7, 8 -1 Favors Banker as high/neutral cards deplete
6 -2 Significant shift toward Banker edge
9, 10, Jack, Queen, King 0 Neutral count effect

The running count updates only after cards are revealed face up.

How to Play

Desktop Controls:

  • Aiming Phase: Move your computer mouse or trackpad across the screen. A dynamic dashed guide vector will follow your path pointing directly toward the hole.
  • Lock Aim: Click once to secure your trajectory line.
  • Power System: The visual Power Bar meter will cycle up and down. Click a second time to select your force capacity and strike the golf ball!
  • Pause: Press the "P" key or click the Pause button on-screen anytime.

Mobile / Tablet Controls:

  • Aiming Phase: Touch and drag your finger across the screen canvas surface to guide your trajectory line.
  • Lock Aim & Shoot: Lift your finger to freeze your aim line, instantly launching the automated Power Bar toggle. Tap the screen once more to lock power and strike!

Core Hazards: Avoid deep sand pits which absorb momentum heavily, and keep away from deep water lakes which wipe out your ball and restart the level!

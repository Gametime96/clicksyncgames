Mastering the Laser Tether Mechanics

Synctime is an intense arcade survival web game that tests your reflexes and spatial awareness. You control a dual-core entity connected by a lethal laser tether. The objective is to survive 12 increasingly difficult levels by slicing through incoming enemy swarms.

The core defense simulator mechanics dictate that your glowing tether is your only weapon, instantly destroying enemies on contact. However, the physical cores at the ends of the tether are highly vulnerable. You must strategically manage your spin direction, expand your radius to strike, and contract it to weave through tight clusters of threats.

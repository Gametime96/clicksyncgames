HOW TO PLAY

• Steer your glowing Yellowfin Tuna using Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) or on-screen controls.

• Eat segmented marine worms to keep your Energy bar filled.

• Avoid dangerous predators patrolling the ocean depths!

• Press P or tap the PAUSE button to pause/unpause anytime.

• Survive 60 seconds to clear each level.

Bad Guy Ladder

Objective: Collect wood bundles and build ladders to climb to the golden escape portal at the top floor!

Controls: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to run and climb. Press Spacebar to drop a structural ladder section beneath you.

Danger: The Bad Guy tracks your position and actively destroys ladders in his path. Don't let him touch you!

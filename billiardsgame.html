Mastering Online Billiards and Pool Physics

Welcome to Classic Billiards, a responsive, browser-based pool simulator designed to test your geometry and cue-ball control. Whether you are practicing for a real-life 8-ball tournament or just looking for a relaxing physics game, our table provides a realistic friction and collision environment.

To succeed, players must carefully calculate the angle of reflection. Remember that hitting the cue ball off-center applies "English" (spin), which alters how the ball reacts off the cushions. Take your time, line up the perfect shot, and clear the table!
